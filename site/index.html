Whois

Who holds a domain or an IP address, read straight from the registry's own records. No ads, no tracking, no account.

Try example.com, 1.1.1.1 or AS3333.

Nothing is sent until you press Look up. Then your browser sends what you typed to IANA (data.iana.org), to find the right registry, and to that registry's RDAP server, which the result names. Nothing goes to this site.

How it works

  
Your browser asks IANA which registry looks after the name or address, then asks that registry over RDAP, the modern replacement for whois. Nothing passes through this site: there is no server here, only these files. The registry sees your request, as it would with any whois tool.

  
Many registries hide personal details of people who hold a domain; you will see "redacted" or a privacy service. Some country registries do not offer RDAP, or do not allow browsers to ask; the page says so when that happens.
